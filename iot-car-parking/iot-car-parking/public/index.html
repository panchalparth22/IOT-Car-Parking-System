✨ Key Features

Everything you need for a smart parking experience

🅿️

12-Slot Smart Parking

Automated slot allocation assigns the nearest available spot when a car arrives. Real-time status tracking for all 12 slots.

🔧

3D Simulation

Fully interactive 3D simulation with cars, gates, sensors and automatic pre-booked car arrivals.

💰

Automated Billing

Flat-rate pricing (£5 direct / £7 pre-book) calculated at exit. Charges deducted from registered wallet balance.

👤

User Management

Sign up, login, manage multiple cars, add payment methods, and track complete parking history.

📊

Real-Time Dashboard

Live parking status, session history, wallet balance, and spending analytics all in one place.

⚡

Automated Gates

Entry gate opens automatically when IR sensor detects a car. Exit gate opens only after successful payment.
